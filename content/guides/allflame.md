Curse of the Allflame runs on one loop: monsters in your maps guard Cursed Treasure that drops **Charts**. You take Charts to **The Sovereign**, descend in the Bathysphere, and clear a seafloor area lit by **Allflame Lanterns**. The first lantern is placed for you; to see further you walk to the edge of the light and place the next one. Lanterns are finite, and placing your last one starts the area collapsing, so you fight your way back up.

Charts you have already run can be slotted onto a **3x3 Voyage board**. Nine of them become a single Voyage: one large area where every Chart's modifiers, the adjacency bonuses, and the rolled **Border Modifiers** all apply at once. Voyages also have Capsules for banking loot mid-run, which Charts do not, and they end by reaching the central Bathysphere under pressure. Voyages are where the league pays out; individual Charts are mostly a means of stocking the board.

The league currency is **Dead Man's Sulphur**, harvested from luminous coral (lanterns help you reach it). Sulphur fuels **Allflame Crafting** at Vesper's bench: item plus a crafting currency plus Sulphur splits the item into ghost outcomes and you pick the one you want. Every craft adds **Intangibility** to that item, cutting how many ghosts you see next time, so the same item gets less predictable the more you work it. Mirrors and Vaal Orbs are locked out of the bench. **Cursed Ducats** only work there.

Ordering for the challenge list: the campaign, act boss, Pantheon quest and Labyrinth rows fall out of levelling. The volume rows arrive on their own if you spec the atlas deliberately and stop skipping the league mechanic: league mechanic counts, master missions, unique maps, Chart types, Border Modifiers and Atlas Anomalies. After that come the ones needing deliberate farming and real investment: voidstones, scarabs, map modifiers, Ducats, astrolabes, Eldritch altars and invitations. The wall is **Brinerot's Bloody Bounty**, **Horrific Halls**, **Apex Assailants** and **Gallant Grinding Goals**: Velka conditionals, corrupted Nightmare Maps, level 85 pinnacles, and the grind picks.

Three things to decide in week one, because they shape everything after: which five mechanics you commit to for **Legendary Leagues**, whether you want the Scion **Luminary** Bloodline class for permanent Mercenaries (it makes **Savage Sellswords** and two **Cross Contamination** rows nearly free), and whether you are chasing 40/40 at all. The scarab and modifier grinds (**Stacked Scarabs**, **Astounding Astrolabes**, **Modified Maps**, **Excruciating Echoes**, **Horrific Halls**) are cheaper if you set your map juicing habits up for them from the start instead of retrofitting in week five.

## Beginner's Basics
difficulty: very-easy
category: early-game

Hillock, Brutus and Merveil are the three mandatory Act 1 bosses. You cannot reach Act 2 without all three, so this completes itself in your first hour.

## Deep Dive
difficulty: very-easy
category: league-mechanics

Do all three the first time the league mechanic offers itself:
- **Visit The Sovereign** happens during the Maiden Voyage introduction quest.
- **Place an Allflame Lantern** is the core movement action underwater; the first lantern in an area is auto-placed, so walk to the edge of the light and drop a second one.
- **Chart an Area** means completing one charted seafloor area, not a full Voyage.

Do not hoard your first Charts waiting for a better board. You need the mechanic unlocked and understood more than you need an optimal first run.

## Peddler's Produce I
difficulty: very-easy
category: early-game

The four quality currencies, all from vendor recipes:
- **Armourer's Scrap**: sell a normal-rarity armour piece at 20% quality, or armour totalling 40% quality.
- **Blacksmith's Whetstone**: same rule with weapons.
- **Glassblower's Bauble**: sell a normal-rarity flask at 20% quality, or flasks totalling 40%.
- **Gemcutter's Prism**: sell a skill gem at 20% quality, or gems totalling 40%.

The gem is the one people miss. Level a spare gem with quality on it, or combine several partially-qualitied gems until one hits 20%, then vendor it. All four are doable before Act 3.

## Act Adversaries I
difficulty: very-easy
category: early-game

Mandatory campaign bosses for Acts 1-5. You get credit when the boss dies even if you died during the fight, so a messy kill still counts.

## Possessive Pantheons
difficulty: easy
category: early-game

These eight are the **Pantheon** quests, not the main storyline, and four of them are easy to run past:
- **The Father of War** (Act 6, Karui Fortress), **The Cloven One** (Act 6, Wetlands), **The Puppet Mistress** (Act 6, Cavern of Anger), **The Master of a Million Faces** (Act 7, Chamber of Sins).
- **Queen of Despair** (Act 8), **Reflection of Terror** (Act 8), **Queen of the Sands** (Act 9), **The Ruler of Highgate** (Act 9).

Check your quest log at the end of each act rather than at the end of the campaign; backtracking to Act 6 zones later is the annoying version of this challenge.

## Underwater Undertaking
difficulty: easy
category: league-mechanics

Four Charts, one Voyage, one Allflame craft. The Voyage is the gate: you need Charts stocked on the board before you can launch one, so run Charts as they drop during the campaign and early maps rather than saving them.

For the craft, use the cheapest thing that qualifies. Any item plus any accepted crafting currency plus the Sulphur cost counts; you do not need a good outcome, just a completed craft. Save your first real Ducat for something that matters instead.

## Act Adversaries II
difficulty: very-easy
category: early-game

Acts 6-10 act bosses, all mandatory. Cap your elemental resistances before Kitava in Act 10 since he applies the second permanent resistance penalty on death of the fight.

## Atlas Accomplishment
difficulty: normal
category: mapping

Every non-unique map on the Atlas, which is the standard full-completion grind. Use Kirac's shop to buy the maps you are missing and his atlas mission to force specific ones. Watch the corners of the Atlas: the tier 1-4 maps you outlevelled in week one are the ones still uncompleted in week four.

Do this early while you are still filling the Atlas anyway. Retrofitting low-tier maps at the end wastes juicing that could have gone into the scarab and modifier challenges.

## Ingrained Increases
difficulty: easy
category: mapping

Three of six, 30 each. **Essences**, **Strongboxes** and **Shrines** are the default answer: all three spawn in maps without any investment, and all three have cheap atlas passive clusters.

**Possessed Monsters** and **Rogue Exiles** are fine alternates if you already specced them, and note that Possessed Monsters overlap with **Cross Contamination**, which wants a Possessed Rogue Exile. **Beyond** is the one to avoid unless you want the atlas points anyway; rare Beyond monsters need density to spawn.

## Legendary Leagues
difficulty: normal
category: mapping

Five of twelve, and this is the pick that shapes your atlas tree for the league, so choose before you spend points.

Cheapest cluster this patch: **Abysses** (the 3.29 rework opens pits automatically instead of making you chase the crawler, so 30 goes fast), **Legion**, **Expeditions**, **Blights** and **Mercenaries**. Mercenaries are core content now and overlap with **Savage Sellswords** and two rows of **Cross Contamination**.

Avoid **Ultimatum Waves x200** and **Rituals x100** unless you enjoy them; both are slow per map. **Harvest Plots x100** is only reasonable if you are already farming Harvest for crafting, though it does feed **Taxing Tasks** tier 4 seeds.

## Trustworthy Teachers
difficulty: normal
category: mapping

Two masters, 10 missions each, in **Tier 16 maps at 80%+ Item Quantity**. Both conditions are checked on the map, so do not burn missions in low tiers; an alch-and-go T16 usually clears 80% IQ on its own.

**Niko** and **Einhar** are the fast picks because their missions resolve inside the map instead of sending you elsewhere. **Alva** costs you a temple run each time and **Jun** ties you to Betrayal board management. Missions spawn from the master atlas passives, so spec the two you picked and stop spending missions on the others.

## Nautical Navigation
difficulty: hard
category: league-mechanics

The real league grind, and all three rows are mandatory:
- **10 Voyages** means 90 completed Charts fed onto the board.
- **200 rare monsters in Voyages** comes along with those Voyages if you pick Border Modifiers that add packs and upgrade monster rarity; **Monsters in Area are at least Magic** and **Area contains additional Monster Packs** both help, and both count for **Fathomless Fears**.
- **50,000 Sulphur spent on equipment crafts** is the slow part. Spend it in many cheap crafts on junk bases rather than saving for perfect items; the counter tracks Sulphur spent, not craft quality.

Because every Voyage consumes nine Charts, treat Chart supply as the bottleneck all league. Take the **Charts have a chance to not be consumed when beginning a Voyage** border modifier whenever it is offered.

## Savage Sellswords
difficulty: normal
category: league-mechanics

Three of four Mercenary rows. **100 rares killed while you have an Allied Mercenary** is the easiest if you take the Scion **Luminary** Bloodline class, which hires mercenaries permanently and stacks up to three; that turns the row into passive progress while mapping.

Without Luminary, hire from Mercenary encounters as they appear and keep one alive. **Warrants and Mercenary Scarabs x15** is a straight purchase-and-run row. **Infamous Modifiers x15** means claiming items carrying those mods when you wager against a mercenary, so take the wager rather than just killing them.

## Chromatic Crafting
difficulty: easy
category: crafting

This challenge changed meaning with the 3.29 socket rework: sockets now default to **White**, gems fit any socket regardless of colour, and coloured sockets roll based on item level. So **15 coloured sockets across your equipment** is no longer automatic; you have to deliberately chromatic your gear into colours.

That pairs with **Use Chromatic Orbs x10** anyway, so do both at once on gear you are about to replace. The **60% gem quality** row wants stacked quality sources: Gemcutter's Prisms take you to 20%, and the new matching-colour socket bonus (+10% quality when the gem colour matches a red, green or blue socket) plus quality from gear and supports gets you the rest. Put your highest-quality gem in a matching socket and check the tooltip.

## Vanquished Villains
difficulty: normal
category: mapping

Four Voidstones, which means the four Pinnacle Atlas bosses: **Searing Exarch**, **Eater of Worlds**, **Maven** and **Uber Elder** (or whichever set your atlas progression puts in front of you). Each is a build check rather than a grind.

Do these on the way to the harder boss challenges instead of as a detour; every one of them also counts toward **Apex Assailants** once you can run them at area level 85, and toward **Bountiful Bloodlines** in some cases. Voidstones raise all map tiers to 16, which the T16-gated challenges (11, 29, 31, 33, 40) need, so this is effectively a prerequisite rather than a side objective.

## Taxing Tasks
difficulty: hard
category: endgame

Five of eleven at area level 81+. The counts are small, so pick by what you already run:
- **Abyssal Depths x5** is the cheapest row this league given the Abyss rework.
- **Blighted or Blight Ravaged Maps x5** and **Fully Completed Ultimatums x10** are self-contained and buyable.
- **Tier 4 Harvest Seeds x5** is trivial if you specced Harvest for **Legendary Leagues**.
- **Claim rewards from Rare Maps completed by your Atlas Runners x30** is passive progress; set runners going every session from day one and this row finishes itself.

Skip **Simulacrum Waves x60** unless your build is already Delirium-proof, and skip **Domains of Timeless Conflict with 25+ rewards** unless you are farming Legion, since 25 rewards needs a well-built Domain.

## Comprehensive Charting
difficulty: normal
category: league-mechanics

Nine of the fourteen Chart types. This is a collection challenge, not a grind: you need one completion of each type, and Charts drop from Cursed Treasure guarded by map monsters.

Keep a stash tab of Charts sorted by type and check which of the fourteen you are missing before you launch a Voyage, since a Voyage eats nine of them. Run a type you have not completed as a standalone Chart for the tick, then use duplicates to fill the board.

## Achieve Ascension
difficulty: easy
category: early-game

The four Labyrinth difficulties plus **Gain a Bloodline Class**, new in 3.29. Normal through Merciless come with campaign progression; Eternal (Uber) Lab needs all six Trials from maps, so pick up Trials as they appear rather than hunting them later. Check poelab.com for the day's layout.

The Bloodline Class is the fifth row and it is a one-off unlock. Two exist this patch, including the Scion **Luminary** which permanently hires Mercenaries; if you take that one you also pick up most of **Savage Sellswords** for free.

## Devious Divination
difficulty: easy
category: crafting

Three of four. **Open Stacked Decks x40** and **Hand in Divination Card sets x30** are both pure volume you can buy your way through, and card sets can be the cheapest full sets on trade; nothing checks which cards you turned in.

**Red maps with a Divination Scarab x10** is fine if you are already running scarabs for **Stacked Scarabs**, since the scarab counts toward both. **Scry a Map** is a single one-off action, so take it if you would rather not commit to the scarab row.

## Peddler's Produce II
difficulty: normal
category: crafting

Three recipes, five completions each. These take real ingredients, unlike the quality currencies in **Peddler's Produce I**:
- **Unique item**: look up a current recipe list before buying anything. A full rare set is the Chaos and Regal recipe, not this one, and the unique recipes get changed between patches.
- **Scarab**: three scarabs of the same name vendor into one of another type.
- **Tier 16 map**: three maps of the same name and tier vendor into one of the next tier, so chain duplicates upward instead of selling them.

Set a stash tab aside for ingredients on day one. Five of each is easy while mapping and tedious as a dedicated session later.

## Challenging Curators
difficulty: normal
category: bossing

Six of eight at level 80+, and most are content you would run anyway:
- **Izaro** at level 80 means Eternal (Uber) Lab, which you need for **Achieve Ascension**.
- **Abyssal Lich** and **Omnitect** come from Abyss and Heist, both of which likely appear in your **Legendary Leagues** picks.
- **Vox Twins** (Sanctum), **K'Tash/Ghorr/Beidat** (Breach), **Medved/Vorana/Uhtred** (Expedition logbook bosses) and **The Black Knight/Admiral Valerius/Sasan** each need only one of the named bosses, so take whichever the mechanic hands you first.

**Uber Atziri** is the one to skip unless your build handles her reflect and flameblast phases; the other seven cover the six you need.

## Ethereal Enhancements
difficulty: hard
category: crafting

Five of nine **Cursed Ducats**. Ducats are league-specific drops that only work at Vesper's Allflame bench, and each one does something normal currency cannot: modifier-preserving copies, attribute shifts, transformations.

Two consequences. First, do not use a Ducat casually on a good item; the challenge only asks that you use it, so spend your challenge Ducats on throwaway bases and save the valuable types for real crafts. Second, if drop RNG gives you four types and never the fifth, buy it; Ducats are tradeable and this challenge does not care where they came from.

Sulphur is the gate as much as the Ducats are, so keep a Sulphur reserve rather than spending everything on the 50,000 counter in **Nautical Navigation**.

## Remarkable Realms
difficulty: normal
category: mapping

Fifteen of eighteen unique maps. Most are cheap to acquire and quick to clear, so buy the fifteen easiest and ignore the rest.

The three to drop: **Hall of the Grandmasters** (a build check that kills most characters), **Vaults of Atziri** and **The Twilight Temple** or **Poorjoy's Asylum** depending on your build. **Acton's Nightmare**, **Mao Kun**, **Oba's Cursed Trove**, **Whakawairua Tuahu**, **Death and Taxes** and **Maelström of Chaos** are all fast.

Run them rare if you are also chasing **Modified Maps**, since unique maps carry explicit modifiers like any other.

## Stacked Scarabs
difficulty: hard
category: grind
calculator: maps with 5 scarabs=5, maps with 3 scarabs=3

750 scarabs applied to maps, counted per scarab rather than per map. Every scarab counts, including the cheapest bulk ones, so this is a buying problem more than a playing problem.

Run five scarabs per map as your default once you can afford it. That habit also satisfies **Astounding Astrolabes** and **Horrific Halls**, both of which require five scarabs applied, and the Divination Scarab row in **Devious Divination**. Buy in bulk rather than singly, and do not save scarabs for a perfect strategy; an applied cheap scarab counts exactly as much as an applied expensive one.

## Corrupted Crystals
difficulty: normal
category: crafting

Three of four gem tasks:
- **Level 10 gems to 20** is passive if you keep a weapon swap loaded with gems you are levelling; rotate finished ones out every few maps.
- **20 corrupted gems socketed** is cheap to buy since corrupted gems are near-worthless in bulk; the socket rework means colour no longer restricts what you can slot, so any 20 corrupted gems fit anywhere.
- **Corrupt a gem to level 21 or 23% quality** is a Vaal Orb gamble; do it on 20/20 gems you can replace.
- **Level a Pact Skill Gem to level 20** needs one of the four new Pact gems, which grant a self-cast buff paired with an affliction burden. Socket it in a swap and let it level even if you never cast it.

Take whichever three are cheapest for you; the Pact gem row is the only one gated on new content.

## Cross Contamination
difficulty: hard
category: endgame

Four of six overlapping-mechanic kills, each a one-off. The trick is setting up conditions that co-occur rather than hoping:
- **Mercenary while Delirious** and **Possessed Rogue Exile while you have an Allied Mercenary** are both easy with the Luminary Bloodline class plus a Delirium Orb; two rows from one setup.
- **Originator Influenced map affected by an Astrolabe** overlaps directly with **Excruciating Echoes** and **Astounding Astrolabes**, so it ticks while you grind those.
- **Abyssal Consort while affected by a Shrine** wants an Abyss map with Shrines specced; both are likely already on your atlas tree from **Ingrained Increases**.
- **Breach Marshal empowered by Wildwood Wisps** and **Legion General while affected by a Tempest** need older mechanics layered in, so take them only if the first four fail.

Spec the atlas for the two mechanics in each pair simultaneously; forcing both to spawn in one map is the whole difficulty.

## Superior Sovereign
difficulty: hard
category: league-mechanics

Five upgrade steps on **The Sovereign**. Nothing separate to chase here: play the league mechanic and the upgrades come with it. Doing it early pays off, since a better ship makes the rest of the league cheaper.

## Bountiful Bloodlines
difficulty: hard
category: bossing

Seven of twelve league bosses. Most are the endgame boss of a past mechanic, so your **Legendary Leagues** picks decide which are cheap:
- Reliably accessible: **Oshabi** (Harvest), **Catarina** (Betrayal), **Olroth** (Expedition logbook, area level 79+), **Simulacrum** (Delirium), **The Trialmaster** (Ultimatum), **Velka** (this league).
- Harder or luck-gated: **Aul** (deep Delve), **Lycia** (Sanctum), **Farrul** (Bestiary spawn), **The King in the Mists**, **Zorath**, **It That Was Tul and It That Was Esh** (both Breach lords in one row).

**Velka** you will fight anyway for **Brinerot's Bloody Bounty**. Aul is the one to drop if you are not already delving; deep Delve is a separate build and time investment. Buying a carry for one or two of these is a normal way to close the gap.

## Astounding Astrolabes
difficulty: very-hard
category: mapping

Six of ten Astrolabe types, five maps each, every map **Tier 16, Rare, with 5 scarabs applied**. That is 30 fully-kitted T16 maps with a specific Astrolabe active, and the scarab requirement makes it the most expensive volume challenge in the league.

Check which Astrolabe you have before applying scarabs, because a wasted five-scarab map is the cost here, not the time. Pick the six types your atlas most reliably produces rather than the six with the best rewards. All 30 maps feed **Stacked Scarabs** at five each (150 of the 750) and **Modified Maps**, so run them rare with as many mods as you can survive and take three challenges from one grind.

## Modified Maps
difficulty: hard
category: grind
calculator: rare maps with 8 mods=8, rare maps with 6 mods=6

3000 explicit modifiers across completed maps. A rare map rolls roughly six to eight explicit mods, so this is about always running rare and never running white or magic maps.

The cheap version: chaos-spam or alch every map to eight mods where your build can take it, which also feeds the 8-modifier row in **Gallant Grinding Goals** and the corrupted-with-8-mods requirement there. Corrupting maps adds mods too. Avoid the temptation to reroll away dangerous mods you can actually handle; each mod you keep is progress, and mod count is the only thing counted here, not tier or quantity.

## Excruciating Echoes
difficulty: hard
category: mapping

Two of four, all at **T16, Rare, 150%+ Item Quantity**. The IQ floor is the real requirement: alch-and-go will not reach 150%, so plan on scarabs, fragments, a Vaal Orb, and IQ-granting map mods together.

**Originator Influenced Maps x30** is the volume row and overlaps with **Cross Contamination**; the other three rows only need five each but are specific map types you have to source. If you can farm Originator influence reliably, take that row plus whichever of **Courtyard of Wasting**, **Chambers of Impunity** or **Theatre of Lies** your atlas gives you. Every map here also counts toward **Stacked Scarabs** and **Modified Maps**.

## Fathomless Fears
difficulty: normal
category: league-mechanics

Nine of sixteen Border Modifiers seen on completed Voyages. Border Modifiers roll on the edges of your Chart board and scale with area level, so higher-level Charts roll a wider pool.

You do not choose these directly, which makes it a tracking exercise: before launching, read the borders and note which of the sixteen are new to you. Because a Voyage costs nine Charts, never abandon one just because the borders are mediocre; a bad-rewards Voyage with two unseen modifiers is still progress. The good news is several of the modifiers are ones you want anyway (extra packs, monsters at least Magic, rares dropping Sulphur or extra scarabs), so this largely completes alongside **Nautical Navigation**.

## Intrigued Interlopers
difficulty: hard
category: endgame

Two of three Eldritch rows in Rare T16 maps, each with an atlas keystone requirement that you have to allocate first:
- **Eldritch Gaze** for the Eater of Worlds row, **Wrath of the Cosmos** for the Searing Exarch row: 4 altars activated with boss-affecting mods, then kill the boss, 10 times each.
- **Destructive Play** for 25 Maven-witnessed maps at 150%+ IQ.

The altar rows are cheaper per map because an influenced map spawns several altars; you need the four you activate to roll modifiers that affect the map boss, so read them rather than clicking through. Take the two altar rows if you are farming Eldritch influence anyway; take the Maven row only if you are already running witnessed maps for **Succinct Summons**, since 150% IQ on 25 separate maps means paying for scarabs or fragments on every one.

## Potent Possibilities
difficulty: hard
category: crafting

Ten of twenty currency types, which means you can ignore the ten worst. **Mirror of Kalandra** is the obvious skip.

The accessible ten: **Tainted Currency x10**, **Catalyst x40**, **Corrupted Essence x4**, **Blight Oil x10**, **Runegraft or Tattoo x5**, **Enshrouding Crystal x5** (new from the Legion rework), **Sacred Crystallised Lifeforce or Crystallised Rancour**, **Veiled Exalted or Chaos Orb**, **Eldritch Exalted/Annulment/Chaos**, and **Exceptional Eldritch Ichor or Ember**. That list is ten on its own and none of it requires chase currency.

If you want a buffer, **Orb of Remembrance/Unravelling/Intention** and **Influenced Exalted Orb** are cheap singles. Use them on junk items the moment you get them; the counter tracks use, not result, and the classic failure here is hoarding a Sacred Orb all league and never clicking it.

## Succinct Summons
difficulty: hard
category: endgame

30 Invitations, each rolled to **5+ explicit modifiers**. Invitations are the Maven's boss-gauntlet items, and the modifier requirement means alching and chaos-spamming each one before you run it.

Roll for mod count, then check the mods against your build; reflect, no-regen and reduced-recovery mods on a 10-boss invitation are how people lose these. The cheaper invitation types are the region ones rather than the 10-boss Maven fights. This overlaps with **Bountiful Bloodlines** and **Apex Assailants** for the bosses inside, so schedule it alongside your pinnacle boss push rather than as its own grind.

## Atlas Assertions
difficulty: normal
category: endgame

Six of eleven **Atlas Anomalies**, the 3.29 encounters that manifest directly on the Atlas without needing a map item. You do not control which appear, so this is opportunistic: run them when they show up rather than saving them.

Because they are free (no map consumed) and each only needs one completion, check the Atlas every session and clear any of the eleven you have not done. These are new in 3.29, so pick by what your build handles rather than by any expected length; six of eleven means you can pass on the three or four that go badly.

## Brinerot's Bloody Bounty
difficulty: very-hard
category: bossing

Three of four conditional kills on **Velka, The Tide Witch**: no hit from **Brine Storm Call**, no hit from **any Anchors**, no hit from **Lightning Whip**, no hit from **Crashing Brine**.

Go for one condition per attempt rather than trying to dodge everything, and drop the fourth entirely. Look up a video guide for her telegraphs before you start.

Bring movement rather than more damage: a rushed kill that clips you once wastes the attempt. You want Velka for **Bountiful Bloodlines** anyway.

## Horrific Halls
difficulty: very-hard
category: endgame

Two of five **Nightmare Maps**, each needing **150%+ Item Quantity, 5 scarabs, Delirium Orbs applied, and corrupted**. Every condition stacks danger on already-hard maps, and the corruption requirement means you cannot reroll a bad outcome.

Order matters: roll the map rare, get your mods and IQ where you want them, apply scarabs and Delirium Orbs, then corrupt last. Pick the two arenas your build handles rather than the two with the best loot, and decide before you corrupt, since that step is irreversible.

Portals are the constraint. Go in with ailment immunity and enough recovery to survive Delirium stacking on top of corrupted map mods, and expect to lose a set or two before it lands. These also count for the Nightmare row in **Gallant Grinding Goals**.

## Apex Assailants
difficulty: very-hard
category: bossing

Five of ten pinnacle bosses at **area level 85**, which is the uber version of each fight. This is the hard damage-and-defence gate of the league.

Easiest five for most builds: **Sirus**, **The Searing Exarch**, **The Eater of Worlds**, **The Maven** and **The Shaper**. **The Elder** and **High Templar Venarius** are step-ups; the three **Incarnations** (Dread, Fear, Neglect) are the Maven-adjacent fights that punish mistakes hardest.

Practice each at normal level first, then invest before attempting 85. Fragments are the cost of failure here, so watch a fight guide for the one or two mechanics that one-shot you rather than learning by dying. Buying a carry for the fifth boss is a legitimate way to finish this, and each kill also feeds **Bountiful Bloodlines** and the pinnacle row of **Gallant Grinding Goals**.

## Gallant Grinding Goals
difficulty: very-hard
category: grind

Four of six, and the choice here decides whether 40/40 takes a week or a month. Pick for overlap with what you have already been doing:
- **Complete T16 Rare Corrupted maps with 8 modifiers x50** overlaps with **Modified Maps**; if you have been running 8-mod rares all league, add corruption and this is nearly done.
- **Defeat Pinnacle bosses at area level 85 x30** overlaps with **Apex Assailants**; once your build can do one uber reliably, 30 repeats is time rather than difficulty.
- **Defeat Rare Monsters in Voyages at area level 83 x400** overlaps with **Nautical Navigation**, but note the higher level floor; you need Voyages at 83, not any Voyage.
- **Complete Nightmare Maps x40** overlaps with **Horrific Halls** but 40 of them is a large ask on its own.

**Reach Level 100** is the best option if your build is safe: 5-way Legion carries or Breachstone farming makes the last levels predictable, and it competes with nothing else. **Cleanse 150 Darkshrines in the Endgame Labyrinth** means repeated Endgame Labyrinth runs, which is slow unless you already farm lab.

Whatever you pick, decide early so your mapping habits build toward it instead of starting fresh at 36/40.
