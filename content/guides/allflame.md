Curse of the Allflame runs on one loop: monsters in your maps guard Cursed Treasure that drops **Charts**. You take Charts to **The Sovereign**, descend in the Bathysphere, and clear a seafloor area lit by **Allflame Lanterns**. The first lantern is placed for you; to see further you walk to the edge of the light and place the next one. Lanterns are finite, and placing your last one starts the area collapsing, so you fight your way back up.

Charts you have already run can be slotted onto a **3x3 Voyage board**. Nine of them become a single Voyage: one large area where every Chart's modifiers, the adjacency bonuses, and the rolled **Border Modifiers** all apply at once. Voyages also have Capsules for banking loot mid-run, which Charts do not, and they end by reaching the central Bathysphere under pressure. Voyages are where the league pays out; individual Charts are mostly a means of stocking the board.

The league currency is **Dead Man's Sulphur**, harvested from luminous coral (lanterns help you reach it). Sulphur fuels **Allflame Crafting** at Vesper's bench: item plus a crafting currency plus Sulphur splits the item into ghost outcomes and you pick the one you want. Every craft adds **Intangibility** to that item, cutting how many ghosts you see next time, so the same item gets less predictable the more you work it. Mirrors and Vaal Orbs are locked out of the bench. **Cursed Ducats** only work there.

Ordering for the challenge list: the campaign, act boss, Pantheon quest and Labyrinth rows fall out of levelling. The volume rows arrive on their own if you spec the atlas deliberately and stop skipping the league mechanic: league mechanic counts, master missions, unique maps, Chart types, Border Modifiers and Atlas Anomalies. After that come the ones needing deliberate farming and real investment: voidstones, scarabs, map modifiers, Ducats, astrolabes, Eldritch altars and invitations. The wall is **Brinerot's Bloody Bounty**, **Horrific Halls**, **Apex Assailants** and **Gallant Grinding Goals**: Velka conditionals, corrupted Nightmare Maps, level 85 pinnacles, and the grind picks.

Three things to decide in week one, because they shape everything after: which five mechanics you commit to for **Legendary Leagues**, whether you want the Scion **Luminary** Bloodline class for permanent Mercenaries (it makes **Savage Sellswords** and two **Cross Contamination** rows nearly free), and whether you are chasing 40/40 at all. The scarab and modifier grinds (**Stacked Scarabs**, **Astounding Astrolabes**, **Modified Maps**, **Excruciating Echoes**, **Horrific Halls**) are cheaper if you set your map juicing habits up for them from the start instead of retrofitting in week five.

## Beginner's Basics
difficulty: very-easy
category: early-game

Hillock, Brutus and Merveil are the three mandatory Act 1 bosses. You cannot reach Act 2 without all three, so this completes easily. :)

## Deep Dive
difficulty: very-easy
category: league-mechanics

Do all three the first time the league mechanic shows up:
- **Visit The Sovereign** happens during the Maiden Voyage introduction quest.
- **Place an Allflame Lantern** is the core movement action underwater; the first lantern in an area is auto-placed, so walk to the edge of the light and drop a second one.
- **Chart an Area** means completing one charted seafloor area, not a full Voyage.

Do not hoard your first Charts waiting for a better board. You need the mechanic unlocked and understood more than you need an optimal first run.

## Peddler's Produce I
difficulty: very-easy
category: early-game

The quality currencies, all from vendor recipes:
- **Armourer's Scrap**: sell a normal-rarity armour piece at 20% quality, or armour totalling 40% quality.
- **Blacksmith's Whetstone**: same rule with weapons.
- **Glassblower's Bauble**: sell a normal-rarity flask at 20% quality, or flasks totalling 40%.
- **Gemcutter's Prism**: sell a skill gem at 20% quality, or gems totalling 40%.

Easy to just buy from trade, and you'll also drop most of these.

## Act Adversaries I
difficulty: very-easy
category: early-game

Mandatory campaign bosses for Acts 1-5. You get credit when the boss dies even if you died during the fight, so a messy kill still counts. Unless you play hardcore, then GG.

## Possessive Pantheons
difficulty: easy
category: early-game

These are the **Pantheon** quests, not the main storyline, and easy to run past:
- **The Father of War** (Act 6, Karui Fortress), **The Cloven One** (Act 6, Wetlands), **The Puppet Mistress** (Act 6, Cavern of Anger), **The Master of a Million Faces** (Act 7, Chamber of Sins).
- **Queen of Despair** (Act 8), **Reflection of Terror** (Act 8), **Queen of the Sands** (Act 9), **The Ruler of Highgate** (Act 9).

Check your quest log at the end of each act, not at the end of the campaign; backtracking to Act 6 zones later is the annoying version of this challenge.

## Underwater Undertaking
difficulty: easy
category: league-mechanics

The Voyage is the gate: you need Charts stocked on the board before you can launch one, so run Charts as they drop during the campaign and early maps. Saving them only delays the Voyage.

For the craft, use the cheapest thing that qualifies. Any item plus any accepted crafting currency plus the Sulphur cost counts; you do not need a good outcome, just a completed craft. Save your first real Ducat for something that matters.

## Act Adversaries II
difficulty: very-easy
category: early-game

Acts 6-10 act bosses, all mandatory. Cap your elemental resistances before Kitava in Act 10, since beating him applies the second permanent resistance penalty.

## Atlas Accomplishment
difficulty: normal
category: mapping

Every non-unique map on the Atlas, which is the standard full-completion grind. Use Kirac's shop to buy the maps you are missing and his atlas mission to force specific ones. Watch the corners of the Atlas: the tier 1-4 maps you outlevelled in week one are the ones still uncompleted in week four.

Do this early while you are still filling the Atlas anyway. Retrofitting low-tier maps at the end wastes juicing that could have gone into the scarab and modifier challenges.

## Ingrained Increases
difficulty: easy
category: mapping

**Essences**, **Strongboxes** and **Shrines** are the default answer: all three spawn in maps without any investment, and all three have cheap atlas passive clusters.

**Possessed Monsters** and **Rogue Exiles** are fine alternates if you already specced them, and note that Possessed Monsters overlap with **Cross Contamination**, which wants a Possessed Rogue Exile. Leave **Beyond** alone unless you want the atlas points anyway; rare Beyond monsters need density to spawn.

## Legendary Leagues
difficulty: normal
category: mapping

This pick shapes your atlas tree for the whole league, so choose before you spend points.

Cheapest cluster this patch: **Abysses** (the 3.29 rework opens pits automatically instead of making you chase the crawler, so 30 goes fast), **Legion**, **Expeditions**, **Blights** and **Mercenaries**. Mercenaries are core content now and overlap with **Savage Sellswords** and two rows of **Cross Contamination**.

Avoid **Ultimatum Waves x200** and **Rituals x100** unless you enjoy them; both are slow per map. **Harvest Plots x100** is only reasonable if you are already farming Harvest for crafting, though it does feed **Taxing Tasks** tier 4 seeds.

## Trustworthy Teachers
difficulty: normal
category: mapping

Every mission has to run in a **Tier 16 map at 80%+ Item Quantity**. Both conditions are checked on the map, so do not burn missions in low tiers; an alch-and-go T16 usually clears 80% IQ on its own.

**Niko** and **Einhar** are the fast picks because their missions resolve inside the map instead of sending you elsewhere. **Alva** costs you a temple run each time and **Jun** ties you to Betrayal board management. Missions spawn from the master atlas passives, so spec the two you picked and stop spending missions on the others.

## Nautical Navigation
difficulty: hard
category: league-mechanics

The real league grind, and every row is mandatory:
- **10 Voyages** means 90 completed Charts fed onto the board.
- **200 rare monsters in Voyages** comes along with those Voyages if you pick Border Modifiers that add packs and upgrade monster rarity; **Monsters in Area are at least Magic** and **Area contains additional Monster Packs** both help, and both count for **Fathomless Fears**.
- **50,000 Sulphur spent on equipment crafts** is the slow part. Spend it in many cheap crafts on junk bases; the counter tracks Sulphur spent, not craft quality, so saving up for perfect items buys you nothing here.

Because every Voyage consumes nine Charts, treat Chart supply as the bottleneck all league. Take the **Charts have a chance to not be consumed when beginning a Voyage** border modifier whenever it is offered.

## Savage Sellswords
difficulty: normal
category: league-mechanics

**100 rares killed while you have an Allied Mercenary** is the easiest row if you take the Scion **Luminary** Bloodline class, which hires mercenaries permanently and stacks up to three; that turns it into passive progress while mapping.

Without Luminary, hire from Mercenary encounters as they appear and keep one alive. **Warrants and Mercenary Scarabs x15** is a straight purchase-and-run row. **Infamous Modifiers x15** means claiming items carrying those mods when you wager against a mercenary, so take the wager; killing them outright gives you nothing here.

## Chromatic Crafting
difficulty: easy
category: crafting

This challenge changed meaning with the 3.29 socket rework: sockets now default to **White**, gems fit any socket regardless of colour, and coloured sockets roll based on item level. So **15 coloured sockets across your equipment** is no longer automatic; you have to deliberately chromatic your gear into colours.

That pairs with **Use Chromatic Orbs x10** anyway, so do both at once on gear you are about to replace. The **60% gem quality** row wants stacked quality sources: Gemcutter's Prisms take you to 20%, and the new matching-colour socket bonus (+10% quality when the gem colour matches a red, green or blue socket) plus quality from gear and supports gets you the rest. Put your highest-quality gem in a matching socket and check the tooltip.

## Vanquished Villains
difficulty: normal
category: mapping

Voidstones require you to complete each atlas quadrant. You can buy a carry or find a public party for this, game should be enough of a guide here.

## Taxing Tasks
difficulty: hard
category: endgame

Everything here happens at area level 81+, can be done without speccing into these, but it's a lot faster if you have an atlas for the picked mechanics. Sharing a Harvest seed etc. is also a possibility, so friends here help, or a community like global 4040.

## Comprehensive Charting
difficulty: normal
category: league-mechanics

A collection challenge, not a grind: one completion of each Chart type, and Charts drop from Cursed Treasure guarded by map monsters.

Keep a stash tab of Charts sorted by type and check which types you are still missing before you launch a Voyage, since a Voyage eats nine of them. Run a type you have not completed as a standalone Chart for the tick, then use duplicates to fill the board.

## Achieve Ascension
difficulty: easy
category: early-game

The Labyrinth difficulties plus **Gain a Bloodline Class**. Normal through Merciless come with campaign progression; Eternal (Uber) Lab needs all six Trials from maps, so pick up Trials as they appear. Hunting them down later is the slow way. Check poelab.com for the day's layout.

The Bloodline Class is not something you have to put points into, you just have to defeat a foe 

## Devious Divination
difficulty: easy
category: crafting

**Open Stacked Decks x40** and **Hand in Divination Card sets x30** are both pure volume you can buy your way through, and card sets can be the cheapest full sets on trade; nothing checks which cards you turned in.

**Red maps with a Divination Scarab x10** is fine if you are already running scarabs for **Stacked Scarabs**, since the scarab counts toward both. **Scry a Map** is a single one-off action, so take it if you would rather not commit to the scarab row.

## Peddler's Produce II
difficulty: normal
category: crafting

These take real ingredients, unlike the quality currencies in **Peddler's Produce I**:
- **Unique item**: look up a current recipe list before buying anything. A full rare set is the Chaos and Regal recipe, not this one, and the unique recipes get changed between patches.
- **Scarab**: three scarabs of the same name vendor into one of another type.
- **Tier 16 map**: three maps of the same name and tier vendor into one of the next tier, so chain duplicates upward instead of selling them.

Set a stash tab aside for ingredients on day one. Collecting as you map is easy; a dedicated session later is tedious.

## Challenging Curators
difficulty: normal
category: bossing

All of these are at level 80+, and most are content you would run anyway:
- **Izaro** at level 80 means Eternal (UBer Uber) Lab.
- **Abyssal Lich** and **Omnitect** come from Abyss and Heist, both of which likely appear in your **Legendary Leagues** picks.
- **Vox Twins** (Sanctum), **K'Tash/Ghorr/Beidat** (Breach), **Medved/Vorana/Uhtred** (Expedition logbook bosses) and **The Black Knight/Admiral Valerius/Sasan** each need only one of the named bosses, so take whichever the mechanic hands you first.

Skip **Uber Atziri** unless your build handles her reflect and flameblast phases; the rest cover everything you need.

You can buy a carry for some of these, so don't sweat it too much.

## Ethereal Enhancements
difficulty: hard
category: crafting

**Cursed Ducats** are league-specific drops that only work at Vesper's Allflame bench, and each type does something normal currency cannot: modifier-preserving copies, attribute shifts, transformations.

You can just spend these on throw away items, or craft something nice for yourself. I usally just play and complete these kinds of tasks last, as I then can force it with currency.

## Remarkable Realms
difficulty: normal
category: mapping

Unique maps, most of them cheap to acquire and quick to clear, so buy the easy ones and ignore the rest.

Drop **Hall of the Grandmasters** (a build check that kills most characters), **Vaults of Atziri**, and **The Twilight Temple** or **Poorjoy's Asylum** depending on your build. **Acton's Nightmare**, **Mao Kun**, **Oba's Cursed Trove**, **Whakawairua Tuahu**, **Death and Taxes** and **Maelström of Chaos** are all fast.

Run them rare if you are also chasing **Modified Maps**, since unique maps carry explicit modifiers like any other.

## Stacked Scarabs
difficulty: hard
category: grind
calculator: maps with 5 scarabs=5, maps with 3 scarabs=3

Counted per scarab, not per map, so the cheapest bulk scarabs count as much as anything else. 

Run five scarabs per map as your default once you can afford it. That habit also satisfies **Astounding Astrolabes** and **Horrific Halls**, both of which require five scarabs applied, and the Divination Scarab row in **Devious Divination**. Buy in bulk, and do not save scarabs for a perfect strategy; an applied cheap scarab counts exactly as much as an applied expensive one.

In general running maps without scarabs is a waste, it's pretty free juice that always pays of.

## Corrupted Crystals
difficulty: normal
category: crafting

The gem tasks:
- **Level 10 gems to 20** is passive if you keep a weapon swap loaded with gems you are levelling; rotate finished ones out every few maps.
- **20 corrupted gems socketed** is cheap to buy since corrupted gems are near-worthless in bulk; the socket rework means colour no longer restricts what you can slot, so any 20 corrupted gems fit anywhere.
- **Corrupt a gem to level 21 or 23% quality** is a Vaal Orb gamble; do it on 20/20 gems you can replace.
- **Level a Pact Skill Gem to level 20** needs one of the four new Pact gems, which grant a self-cast buff paired with an affliction burden. Socket it in a swap and let it level even if you never cast it.

Take whichever are cheapest for you; the Pact gem row is the only one gated on new content.

## Cross Contamination
difficulty: hard
category: endgame

Overlapping-mechanic kills, each a one-off. Set up conditions that co-occur; waiting for them to happen by accident does not work:
- **Mercenary while Delirious** and **Possessed Rogue Exile while you have an Allied Mercenary** are both easy with the Luminary Bloodline class plus a Delirium Orb; two rows from one setup.
- **Originator Influenced map affected by an Astrolabe** overlaps directly with **Excruciating Echoes** and **Astounding Astrolabes**, so it ticks while you grind those.
- **Abyssal Consort while affected by a Shrine** wants an Abyss map with Shrines specced; both are likely already on your atlas tree from **Ingrained Increases**.
- **Breach Marshal empowered by Wildwood Wisps** and **Legion General while affected by a Tempest** need older mechanics layered in, so take them only if the first four fail.

Spec the atlas for the two mechanics in each pair simultaneously; some can also be done with scarabs.

## Superior Sovereign
difficulty: hard
category: league-mechanics

Upgrade steps on **The Sovereign**. Nothing separate to chase: play the league mechanic and the upgrades come with it. Doing it early pays off, since a better ship makes the rest of the league cheaper. **Velka** is the last upgrade.

## Bountiful Bloodlines
difficulty: hard
category: bossing

League bosses, most of them the endgame boss of a past mechanic, so your **Legendary Leagues** picks decide which are cheap:
- Reliably accessible: **Oshabi** (Harvest), **Catarina** (Betrayal), **Olroth** (Expedition logbook, area level 79+), **Simulacrum** (Delirium), **The Trialmaster** (Ultimatum), **Velka** (this league).
- Harder or luck-gated: **Aul** (deep Delve), **Lycia** (Sanctum), **Farrul** (Bestiary spawn), **The King in the Mists**, **Zorath**, **It That Was Tul and It That Was Esh** (both Breach lords in one row).

**Velka** you will fight anyway for **Brinerot's Bloody Bounty**. 

You can buy an "Aul taxi" but the host can not help you to kill aul, just remember flairs.

## Astounding Astrolabes
difficulty: very-hard
category: mapping

Five maps per Astrolabe type, and every one has to be **Tier 16, Rare, with 5 scarabs applied**. That runs to 30 fully-kitted T16 maps,.

All 30 maps feed **Stacked Scarabs** at five each and **Modified Maps** as well, so run them rare with as many mods as you can survive and take three challenges from one grind.

## Modified Maps
difficulty: hard
category: grind
calculator: rare maps with 8 mods=8, rare maps with 6 mods=6

A rare map rolls six to eight (vaaled) explicit mods, so this is about always running rare and never running white or magic maps.

**Gallant Grinding Goals** benefit from this as well, so alch n vaal n go. Pairs well with other map challenges.

## Excruciating Echoes
difficulty: hard
category: mapping

Everything here is **T16, Rare, 150%+ Item Quantity**, and the IQ floor is what actually hurts: alch-and-go will not reach 150%, so plan on scarabs, fragments, a Vaal Orb, and IQ-granting map mods together.

**Originator Influenced Maps x30** is the volume row and overlaps with **Cross Contamination**; the other rows need far fewer maps but are specific types you have to source. If you can farm Originator influence reliably, take that row plus whichever of **Courtyard of Wasting**, **Chambers of Impunity** or **Theatre of Lies** your atlas gives you. Every map here also counts toward **Stacked Scarabs** and **Modified Maps**.

## Fathomless Fears
difficulty: normal
category: league-mechanics

Border Modifiers roll on the edges of your Chart board and scale with area level, so higher-level Charts roll from a wider pool. They only count on Voyages you finish.

You do not choose these directly, which makes it a tracking exercise: before launching, read the borders and note which ones are new to you. Because a Voyage costs nine Charts, never abandon one just because the borders are mediocre; a bad-rewards Voyage with two unseen modifiers is still progress. Several of the modifiers are ones you want anyway (extra packs, monsters at least Magic, rares dropping Sulphur or extra scarabs), so this largely completes alongside **Nautical Navigation**.

## Intrigued Interlopers
difficulty: hard
category: endgame

Eldritch rows in Rare T16 maps, each with an atlas keystone you have to allocate first:
- **Eldritch Gaze** for the Eater of Worlds row, **Wrath of the Cosmos** for the Searing Exarch row: 4 altars activated with boss-affecting mods, then kill the boss, 10 times each.
- **Destructive Play** for 25 Maven-witnessed maps at 150%+ IQ.

I find that using an Scarab with influenced packsize gives a bit more chance on the right 4 alters you need. But this can be pretty painful. It can be shared with friends, so this would speed it up a lot.

## Potent Possibilities
difficulty: hard
category: crafting

You can ignore half the list, and **Mirror of Kalandra** is the obvious skip.

The accessible ones: **Tainted Currency x10**, **Catalyst x40**, **Corrupted Essence x4**, **Blight Oil x10**, **Runegraft or Tattoo x5**, **Enshrouding Crystal x5** (new from the Legion rework), **Sacred Crystallised Lifeforce or Crystallised Rancour**, **Veiled Exalted or Chaos Orb**, **Eldritch Exalted/Annulment/Chaos**, and **Exceptional Eldritch Ichor or Ember**. None of that requires chase currency.

If you want a buffer, **Orb of Remembrance/Unravelling/Intention** and **Influenced Exalted Orb** are cheap singles. Use them on junk items the moment you get them.

## Succinct Summons
difficulty: hard
category: endgame

Every Invitation has to be rolled to **5+ explicit modifiers**. Invitations are the Maven's boss-gauntlet items, so that means alching or chaos-spamming each one before you run it.

Roll for mod count, then check the mods against your build; reflect, no-regen and reduced-recovery mods ect.

This overlaps with **Bountiful Bloodlines** and **Apex Assailants** for the bosses inside, so schedule it alongside your pinnacle boss push.

## Atlas Assertions
difficulty: normal
category: endgame

**Atlas Anomalies** are the 3.29 encounters that manifest directly on the Atlas without needing a map item. You do not control which appear, so this is opportunistic: run them when they show up.

Check the Atlas every session and clear anything you have not done. They are new in 3.29, so pick by what your build handles; you can pass on the few that go badly. I find them pretty easy and some pretty fun. Can be shared with friends.

## Brinerot's Bloody Bounty
difficulty: very-hard
category: bossing

Conditional kills on **Velka, The Tide Witch**: no hit from **Brine Storm Call**, no hit from **any Anchors**, no hit from **Lightning Whip**, no hit from **Crashing Brine**.

Look up a video guide for telegraphs before you start.

Bring movement over more damage: a rushed kill that clips you once wastes the attempt. You want Velka for **Bountiful Bloodlines** anyway.

## Horrific Halls
difficulty: very-hard
category: endgame

**Nightmare Maps** needing **150%+ Item Quantity, 5 scarabs, Delirium Orbs applied, and corrupted**. Every condition stacks danger on already-hard maps, and the corruption requirement means you cannot reroll a bad outcome.

Order matters: roll the map rare, get your mods and IQ where you want them, apply scarabs and Delirium Orbs, then corrupt last. Pick the arenas your build handles, not the ones with the best loot, and decide before you corrupt, since that step is irreversible.

Also count for the Nightmare row in **Gallant Grinding Goals**.

## Apex Assailants
difficulty: very-hard
category: bossing

Pinnacle bosses at **area level 85**, the uber version of each fight, and the hard damage-and-defence gate of the league.

Easiest for most builds: **Sirus**, **The Searing Exarch**, **The Eater of Worlds**, **The Maven** and **The Shaper**. **The Elder** and **High Templar Venarius** are step-ups; the three **Incarnations** (Dread, Fear, Neglect) are the Maven-adjacent fights that punish mistakes hardest.

## Gallant Grinding Goals
difficulty: very-hard
category: grind

**Defeat Pinnacle bosses at area level 85 x30** can be done pretty quickly with a good build and invitations. If you are going for 40/40 your main build should be able to handle it.

**Complete T16 Rare Corrupted maps with 8 modifiers x50** Should be very easy, just complete some maps, you can rush Mesa or City Square.

**Defeat Rare Monsters in Voyages at area level 83 x400** This one is free, it should be completed very quickly.

**Complete Nightmare Maps x40** You can boss rush these white if you don't feel like doing more with them.

**Reach Level 100** If you play enough, have a tanky build or just buy level carries, then this is also pretty doable. But can be boring.
