## Beginner's Basics
difficulty: very-easy
category: early-game
timeInvestment: minimal

Cheap orbs you will have from the campaign. Crafting bench uses count too.

## Witch's Wishes
difficulty: very-easy
category: early-game
timeInvestment: minimal

Comes on its own from playing the league mechanic early.

## Peddler's Produce I
difficulty: very-easy
category: early-game
timeInvestment: minimal

All four come from vendor recipes:
- **Life Flask**: sell three Life Flasks of the same base type (e.g. three Small Life Flasks) to get one of the next base type.
- **Mana Flask**: same with three Mana Flasks.
- **Ruby, Topaz or Sapphire Ring**: sell an **Iron Ring** with a red, green or blue gem. Red gives Ruby, green gives Topaz, blue gives Sapphire; any one of them counts.
- **Agate, Turquoise or Citrine Amulet**: sell two different amulets out of **Amber**, **Jade** and **Lapis**, plus an **Orb of Transmutation**. Amber + Lapis gives Agate, Jade + Lapis gives Turquoise, Amber + Jade gives Citrine.

## Act Adversaries I
difficulty: very-easy
category: early-game
timeInvestment: short

Mandatory campaign bosses for Acts 1-5. You get credit when the boss dies even if you died during the fight, so a messy kill still counts. Unless you play hardcore, then GG.

## Gifted Gods
difficulty: very-easy

Engage the league mechanic before the minor Pantheon gods in Acts 6-9 so they get a Fate. It stops spawning if you outlevel the zone. Each god's map boss equivalent also counts.

## Equipped Effigy
difficulty: very-easy

Viscera drop from fated bosses; this fills up while playing the league.

## Act Adversaries II
difficulty: very-easy
category: early-game
timeInvestment: short

Acts 6-10 act bosses, all mandatory. Cap your elemental resistances before Kitava in Act 10, since beating him applies the second permanent resistance penalty.

## Atlas Additions
difficulty: easy

None of these can be forced with Threads. Spec the matching chance nodes on the Atlas tree and respec between them. Surpassing Chance above 100% gives extra spawns per map.

## Atlas Authorities
difficulty: very-easy

Run the matching Thread for each mechanic. No tier requirement.

## Foretold Foes
difficulty: very-easy

No tier requirement and one Fate is enough, so low maps work.

## Intrepid Investigator
difficulty: very-easy

Normal atlas completion.

## Agonising Adversaries
difficulty: easy

Pick the five that fit your tree:
- **Possessed Map Bosses**: take `Unrelenting Torment` and let the spirit reach the boss
- **Infamous Mercenaries**: spec Mercenaries, or buy them on trade
- **Apparition or Evolving Shrines** and **Possessed Rogue Exiles**: take the spawn and upgrade nodes
- **Unique Strongboxes**: strongbox nodes give several per map

## Fundamental Foes
difficulty: easy

Area level 81 is tier 14. Easy picks:
- **Queen Atziri**: run a `Chronicle of Atzoatl`, the non-uber one is fine
- **Logbooks**: any level 81+ logbook gets 10 remnants without trying
- **Ultimatums**, **Breach Marshals/Hive Fortresses**, **Safehouses**: use the matching Thread

## Sigil Slayer
difficulty: easy

Completes itself while mapping red maps with the league mechanic.

## Creative Currencies
difficulty: very-easy

Skip the expensive ones: Divine, Fracturing, Sacred, Hinekora's Lock and Mirror.

## Vital Voidstones
difficulty: normal
category: mapping
timeInvestment: moderate

Comes with normal atlas progression.

## Achieve Ascension
difficulty: easy
category: early-game
timeInvestment: short

All four labs plus one bloodline boss. See Legacied Lineage for the bloodline options.

## Promised Profits
difficulty: easy

Keep killing fated bosses; no area level requirement. The rewards add up while mapping.

## Dubious Divination
difficulty: very-easy

Open Stacked Decks as you find them and turn in cheap common sets. Chaotic Disposition is often a cheap set later in the league.

## Peddler's Produce II
difficulty: very-easy

- **Tier 16 Map**: three tier 15s
- **Unique Item**: three copies of the same non-corrupted unique
- **Item with Influence**: influenced Iron Ring plus any coloured gem
- **Exalted Orb**: full set of rares with the same influence (unidentified gives two)

## Mysterious Mirages
difficulty: easy

Max Mirage chance on the Atlas tree. Mirages are full maps now, and you cannot open a new portal to leave one.

## Vulgar Viscera
difficulty: easy

Heads come from atlas bosses and Nightmare maps. Buying them is much faster than farming.

## Unequaled Uniques
difficulty: normal
category: mapping
timeInvestment: short

Skip Hall of Grandmasters and Doryani's Machinarium. For The Vinktar Square, vendor the four Agnerod staffs to get the map.

## Tangled Threads
difficulty: easy

Every applied Thread counts: roughly 42 maps at max Threads, 125 at minimum. Overlaps with Woven Wonders and Grand Goal Grind.

## Complicit Corruption
difficulty: easy

Do Vaal Orbs, Tainted Currency (use the cheapest type) and the corrupted-item divination sets. Skip Volatile Vaal Orbs and double corrupts.

## Brilliant Branding
difficulty: normal

Tier 3 Fates show three feathers top left; you get them by playing two or three of the same named Fate. Needs tier 16 with 100%+ Atlas Drops.

## Cross Contamination
difficulty: hard
category: endgame
timeInvestment: moderate

Mostly: spec one mechanic on the Atlas tree and run the other with its Thread.
- Shrines + Betrayal, Strongboxes + Blight, Essences + Bestiary
- **Chaplain of the Well**: Abyss Threads
- **Possessed Runic monster** has no reliable method

## Legacied Lineage
difficulty: hard
category: bossing
timeInvestment: moderate

Pick seven. Most fights open with a bought item: King in the Mists, Oshabi, Catarina, Farrul and the Breachlords (Hivebrain Gland). Simulacrum is hard for most builds. Scarabs are gone, so The Trialmaster can no longer be forced with one.

## Adjacent Anomalies
difficulty: normal

Betrayal interrogations can spawn Syndicate anomalies; most players finish it this way. Anomaly chance nodes are also on the Atlas tree. Anomalies can be shared in a party.

## Overpowering Omens
difficulty: hard

Tier 16 with 100%+ Atlas Drops and a tier 3 Fate. Twist of Fate and Shaping the Valleys help reach the drops threshold. Getting the tier 3 Fate is the slow part.

## Atlas Adventure
difficulty: normal

Roughly 150-300 juiced maps. Stack modifier effect on the tree.

## Otherworldly Obstructions
difficulty: easy

Use Maven's destructive play on a boss with phases to cut down the Maven count.

## Amazingly Accomplished Atlas
difficulty: normal

Full atlas: all invitations, pinnacles and 10 unique maps. Carries work.

## Astounding Augury
difficulty: very-hard

Grim Fates are rare, untradeable drops from the league mechanic in maps, used from the boss fight UI. Uber kills count for this and Ultimate Undertaking, so five different Grim Fates covers both. Carries work.

## Zana's Zealotry
difficulty: easy

About 50 Originator maps. No tier requirement.

## Woven Wonders
difficulty: normal

Tier 14+ with 8 Threads of one type and 100%+ Atlas Drops. Bestiary Thread modifiers are dangerous; skip it if you can.

## Nightmarish Nests
difficulty: normal

Prefixes can't be removed any more, so roll or buy maps your build can handle.

## Ultimate Undertaking
difficulty: very-hard

Uber versions of the Astounding Augury bosses at area level 85. Finding the Grim Fates is the bottleneck. Carries work.

## Grand Goal Grind
difficulty: hard
timeInvestment: long

Pick four:
- **Level 100**: buy five-way runs. Omen of Amelioration saves your XP once per area; don't bring it into an invitation or uber fight, it gets used up anyway
- **Uber Lab font**: Gift to the Goddess gives 8 uses per run
- **6-mod rares**: take `Anomie` and run T16.5 or Nightmare maps with +1 rare mod. Legion Threads or Bismuth ore maps can give 20+ per map
- **8-Thread T16 maps**: overlaps with Woven Wonders and Tangled Threads
