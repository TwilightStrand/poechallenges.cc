## Peddler's Produce I
difficulty: very-easy
category: early-game
timeInvestment: minimal

All four come from vendor recipes:
- **Life Flask**: sell three Life Flasks of the same base type (e.g. three Small Life Flasks) to get one of the next base type.
- **Mana Flask**: same with three Mana Flasks.
- **Ruby, Topaz or Sapphire Ring**: sell an **Iron Ring** with a red, green or blue gem. Red gives Ruby, green gives Topaz, blue gives Sapphire; any one of them counts.
- **Agate, Turquoise or Citrine Amulet**: sell two different amulets out of **Amber**, **Jade** and **Lapis**, plus an **Orb of Transmutation**. Amber + Lapis gives Agate, Jade + Lapis gives Turquoise, Amber + Jade gives Citrine.

## Act Adversaries I
difficulty: very-easy
category: early-game
timeInvestment: short

Mandatory campaign bosses for Acts 1-5. You get credit when the boss dies even if you died during the fight, so a messy kill still counts. Unless you play hardcore, then GG.
